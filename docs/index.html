Originle game tip: separate language from nationality

Originle is a daily game where you watch short clips of a real guest and guess which country they are from (up to 3 guesses, 8 clues). Free to play.

Accent and spoken language narrow a region, but diaspora and travel footage mean language alone is not a final answer. Treat it as a soft filter, then wait for place-specific props (signs, brands, plates, vegetation).

Official play vs fan companion

Play the clips on originle.io. For clue titles, archive browsing, and score notes, use the unofficial fan companion originle.app — it is not the official Originle site.
